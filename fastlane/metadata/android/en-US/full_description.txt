LoReLy (Low Res Rally) is a minimalist retro-style 3D rally game with its own driving physics.

Six worlds: Snow, City, Beach, Rhine, Offroad and Suburb. Four cars with different handling; ice, sand, mud, gravel and cobblestones change the grip.

Two modes: Time Trial (best time per car) and Collect (gather the stars).

The app runs fully offline, needs no permissions and has no tracking, no ads and no accounts. Free and open source under the MIT license, built with three.js.
